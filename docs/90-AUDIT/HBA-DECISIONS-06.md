**HBA statements (2026-10-05), plain reading:**

- A Manager-approved WO means CLOSED. A CLOSED WO holding photo A stays as is.
- Changing photo A to photo B after CLOSED requires a revision. After the
  revision is approved by the Manager, what is shown is photo B.

  remains viewable.
